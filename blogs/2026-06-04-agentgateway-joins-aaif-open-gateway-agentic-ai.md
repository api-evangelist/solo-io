Agentgateway has been accepted as the fourth hosted project under the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), operating under Linux Foundation governance, providing unified infrastructure for managing MCP traffic, agent-to-agent communication, LLM inference, and traditional APIs. With over 300 active contributors across 60+ organizations, the project addresses governance, observability, routing, and security challenges that arise when agentic systems move into production environments.
